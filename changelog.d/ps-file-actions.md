### Features

- **Send to Paperless and Open in Paperless in Files:** the menu of a file or a folder in Files, and of a selection, has *Send to Paperless*: the file goes to Paperless with the tags of the inbox, those of its subfolders below a sent folder included, and stays where it is, and a notification tells you when Paperless has it, with a link to the new document, or why Paperless didn't import it. A file whose content Paperless has already isn't uploaded again, so Paperless-ngx 3.x makes no duplicate. The uploads use the API token of the app, so only the members of *Group that may send files* in *4 · Nextcloud inbox* get the action: the administrators, until you choose another group or none; the connection checklist then tests that the account of Paperless may add documents. *Open in Paperless* opens the document of every copy of the archive, by the marker such as `[P123]` in its name, for everyone who sees the file.
