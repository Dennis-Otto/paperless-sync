### Features

- **A request that Paperless refuses during a run says what to do.** When Paperless refuses the API token, the account lacks a permission, a document went away during the run, Paperless or its proxy fails, or Nextcloud can't reach Paperless, can't resolve its name, waits too long or doesn't trust its certificate, the status and the report on the settings page explain in your language what happened and how to fix it, and the notification and the status API in English, instead of the bare English message of the HTTP client, which stays as a detail and in the log. The box of the errors links to the table *Messages and their fixes* of the troubleshooting page, which has a row for each of them. A Paperless without version 9 of its API is named in your language too.
