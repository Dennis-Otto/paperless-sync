### Bug fixes

- **A document that Paperless still has keeps its copy, also when a scan left it out:** when one document was deleted in Paperless and another one added while a run read the pages of its list, the count of Paperless stayed the same, and a third document that still exists could be left out without a sign. That counted as a missing scan, and with *Required consecutive missing scans* at 1 its copy moved to the deleted folder, or was deleted with direct deletion. A run now asks Paperless for each document that a scan would count as missing: only when Paperless answers that it has no such document does the scan count; a document that Paperless still has keeps its copy and starts its count anew, and an error of Paperless counts nothing in that run and shows in the report.
