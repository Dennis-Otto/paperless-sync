### Bug fixes

- **A user of Nextcloud whose ID is all digits works like any other**, such as `1001` from LDAP. Before, every run with per-user ownership, with the documents that Paperless shares or with "Tags that a user may see" failed with an internal type error that named no setting as soon as such a user, also the target user, got documents or had a line, and the connection checklist warned that Nextcloud could not store their files. Now they get their documents, their inbox and their report as every other user does.
