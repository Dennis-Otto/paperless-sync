### Bug fixes

- **A copy in the deleted folder no longer moves into a second one when its document shows up in the trash of Paperless.** A document that the missing scans had moved to `_Deleted/<day>/` and that then appeared in the trash of Paperless went to `_Deleted/<day>/_Deleted/<day>/…`. Now the copy stays where it is, and the run only marks the document as trashed.
- **The switches *Prefer Paperless archive versions* and *Skip Paperless inbox documents* explain themselves.** Each has a hint, which a screen reader reads with it, and a link to the guide: what an archive version is and what the originals bring instead, such as images or office documents and scans without a layer of text, and that the inbox there is that of Paperless, not the Nextcloud inbox, so a document with an inbox tag of Paperless waits until it is filed there.
