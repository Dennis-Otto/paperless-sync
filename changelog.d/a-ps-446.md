### Features

- **Links to Paperless work when Nextcloud reaches Paperless by a name of Docker or of your network.** The new optional setting *Address of Paperless in the browser* in *1 · Connection and ownership* holds the address that browsers open, such as `https://paperless.example.com`, while Nextcloud keeps sending its requests to the Paperless URL, such as `http://paperless:8000` in the network of Docker Compose. *Open in Paperless* and *Open Paperless* in Files, the notification of a file sent from Files, the entry of an import in the Activity app and the `url` of the metadata next to the archive take it; left empty, they take the Paperless URL as before. The next run writes the metadata once more with the new address.
