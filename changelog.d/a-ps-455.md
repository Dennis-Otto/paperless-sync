### Bug fixes

- **Tags that a user may see** now also finds a share of the archive with a Team that a user with tags is in, which shows them every document in it, as a share with a group does. A share with a conversation of Talk or a board of Deck, whose members Nextcloud doesn't name, is listed as one that may reach a user with tags: the connection checklist warns of it instead of passing, and the settings page and `occ paperless_sync:status` list it too. Without Talk, Deck or Teams, the check works as before.
