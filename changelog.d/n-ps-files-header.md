### Features

- **Files says what the folders of Paperless Sync do:** a note above the list in the archive and below it says that the folder is a mirror of Paperless, when it was last updated, and that changes there don't reach Paperless, with a link to Paperless. In the inbox, it says when the files go to Paperless, with a link to the failed imports, and there it says that the reason of each failure is in the `.error.txt` next to the file. People who reach a folder through a share see it too. Each person can hide each kind of the note, and *What is this folder?* brings it back.
