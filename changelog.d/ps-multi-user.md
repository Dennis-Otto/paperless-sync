### Features

- **Every user their own documents:** with *Who gets the documents* in *1 · Connection and ownership* set to *Each user, those they own in Paperless*, each user of Nextcloud gets the documents that their user of Paperless owns, in the same folders below the base folder of their own files: by the same ID, whatever its case, as single sign-on such as OpenID Connect gives them, or by the table *Paperless users with another Nextcloud ID*, and only for the members of a group if you choose one. The files of a user's inbox, and those they send with *Send to Paperless*, become theirs in Paperless and come back into their archive. The target user gets the documents without an owner and those of users of Paperless without a user of Nextcloud, so that none is lost; two users of Paperless for one user of Nextcloud get neither, and the connection checklist shows who gets which documents, with their numbers. When the owner of a document changes in Paperless, its copy moves to the archive of the new owner. Each user has their own batch, Activity entries and notifications, and the report counts the changes of each user. The account of the app needs to see the documents of every user and to view the users of Paperless; the security design explains how. The default, the target user for all documents, keeps your archive as it is ([#84](https://github.com/Dennis-Otto/paperless-sync/issues/84)).
