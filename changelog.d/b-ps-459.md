### Features

- **Write all copies again from the settings page.** A new button beside *Refresh* does what `occ paperless_sync:forget --all` does, for administrators without a shell, such as at a hoster of managed Nextcloud, and for the members of a group to which the page is delegated: after a question that says what it costs, the next runs download every document again, batch by batch, and write it in place of its copy. A copy that someone edited or that was damaged in Nextcloud gets the content of Paperless back, and keeps its path, its shares and its versions.

### Bug fixes

- **A large document over a slow line is no longer cut off after three minutes.** The download of a document from Paperless and the upload of a file of the inbox stopped after 180 seconds, however much was still arriving, so a large scan over a slow uplink or a VPN failed in every run. Now a transfer goes on as long as it moves, and stops only after a minute below 1 KB a second, or after an hour. One that stops says so, with the document and the bytes that arrived, or with the file and its size, and the troubleshooting page explains it.
