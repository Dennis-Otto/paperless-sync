### Bug fixes

- **A failed inbox file never replaces another one in the error folder.** When the name is taken there, as when a scanner names every scan `scan.pdf` and two of them fail, the second one takes the next free number, `scan (2).pdf`, with its own `.error.txt`, whatever the conflict policy. Before, the default policy *Replace* deleted the earlier failed file, the only original of it, and overwrote its reason, and the policy *Skip* kept the second file in the inbox with a conflict.
