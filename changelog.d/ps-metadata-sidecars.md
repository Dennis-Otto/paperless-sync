### Features

- **Metadata next to the archive:** with *Write metadata next to the archive* in *3 · Structured archive*, every run writes what Paperless knows of each document into the hidden folder `.paperless` of the archive: `P123.json` with the title, the correspondent, the document type, the tags, the notes, the custom fields, the dates and the address in Paperless, and `index.csv` with a row for every document and the path of its copy, which LibreOffice Calc and Excel open as it is. So the archive describes itself also without Paperless, such as in a backup of Nextcloud or on the way to another system. A sidecar is written only when its content changes, follows its copy into the deleted folder, and goes with it under the rules of deletion safety. It is off until you turn it on.
